About Miss Maya

Private tutor

Miss Maya Tutoring Services is a private maths tutoring service based in Graaf-Reinett in the Eastern Cape.

Miss Maya helps learners understand maths instead of just memorising it, so confidence grows together with marks.

Every learner starts in a different place. Lessons begin with what the learner already knows, work step by step through what feels difficult, and finish with practice that shows real progress.

Whether it is Mathematics or Mathematical Literacy, one-on-one or in a group, for any grade, the goal is the same: a learner who is not afraid of the next question.
